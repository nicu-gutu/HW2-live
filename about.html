Our Mission

Founded with the vision of creating a more connected campus, StudLeif exists to bridge the gap between students and the incredible opportunities around them. In 2026, our goal is to ensure that every student finds their community, develops new skills, and builds lasting memories. Whether it's finding your first coding mentor or simply discovering a new hobby, StudLeif is the bridge between you and the vibrant community waiting for you on campus.

The StudLeif Story

What started as a small student council initiative has grown into the central hub for campus life. Over the past few semesters, we have successfully facilitated hundreds of events and helped launch dozens of student-led organizations. Today, our digital portal makes campus engagement more accessible than ever before.

We believe that learning extends far beyond the lecture halls, and our platform is built to support that philosophy by promoting inclusivity, leadership, and collaboration.

What We Offer

Verified clubs: An official ensemble of student-led organizations.

Dynamic Calendar: Up-to-the-minute updates on workshops, social gatherings, and forums.

Centralized Info: Room assignments, meeting times, and attendance requirements all in one place.

Where to Find Us

While our digital portal is available 24/7, we always welcome students to drop by in person. Whether you want to pitch a new club idea or just need help navigating the event schedule, our team is here to help.

  • Office Location: Student Union Building, Room 204
  • Walk-in Hours: Monday - Thursday, 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM
  • Virtual Support: Fridays via video call (booking available in the student portal)
