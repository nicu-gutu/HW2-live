Attendance Information

Before attending, please review our standard event guidelines:

  • Student ID: You must present your valid 2026 Campus ID at the door.
  • Booking: For events hosted in the Grand Hall, please book your attendance via your student portal at least 48 hours in advance to guarantee a seat.
  • Arrival: We recommend arriving 5-10 minutes early to grab a seat and introduce yourself to the coordinators.
  • Membership: Attending a weekly meeting is free! Formal membership sign-ups usually happen at the end of each month.
